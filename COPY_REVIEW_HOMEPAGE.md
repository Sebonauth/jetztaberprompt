# Homepage copy review

Scope: conversion-relevant homepage copy. Navigation labels, prices, dates, legal
copy, and most factual bullet points are excluded.

Review criteria: clarity, specificity, active voice, credibility, audience focus,
and consistent German/English terminology.

## Recommended review order

1. Hero: C01–C03
2. Core positioning: C04–C09
3. Course cards: C10–C18
4. Trust and format: C19–C21
5. About and conversion: C22–C27
