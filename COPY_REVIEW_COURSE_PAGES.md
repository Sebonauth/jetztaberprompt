# Course detail page copy review

Scope: high-impact, visible copy on all four course detail pages. Navigation,
dates, prices, metadata, legal language, and factual agenda or deliverable bullets
are excluded unless they conflict with the positioning already approved on the
homepage.

Review criteria: clarity, specificity, active voice, credibility, audience fit,
consistent German/English terminology, and consistency across homepage and
course pages.
