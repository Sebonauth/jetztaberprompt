## What Works Well

1. Strong personal expert positioning.
   The site immediately shows a name, a clear specialization, and a real person. This builds trust faster than abstract UI alone.

2. Large editorial hero with one highlighted keyword.
   The H1 is simple, oversized, and memorable. The orange-highlighted keyword makes the proposition scannable without adding extra copy.

3. Lean CTA structure.
   The hero has one obvious primary action, not several competing buttons.

4. Factual proof shelf.
   The metrics row works because it is concrete and visually compact. It creates authority without long testimonials.

5. Clear expertise taxonomy.
   The site separates expertise areas from services and workshops. That helps visitors understand both "what this person knows" and "what I can buy."

6. Service cards with restrained borders and icons.
   The cards are calm, easy to scan, and not over-decorated. Thin green border accents give them structure.

7. Detailed workshop cards.
   The workshop cards combine outcome copy, bullets, dates/status, and detail CTAs in one repeatable product block.

8. Dark final contact section.
   The closing CTA has stronger contrast than the page body and gives the end of the page a clear conversion moment.

## Components Worth Adapting For JetztAberPROMPT!

1. Hero with a real profile signal.
   If Sebastian has a usable photo, bring it into the hero or "Warum Sebastian" section. If not, use a generated branded visual, but do not fake a testimonial/client context.

2. One-word hero emphasis.
   Keep the current JetztAberPROMPT! warmth, but highlight one strategic term, for example "KI", "Business", or "Workflows", instead of coloring many fragments.

3. Compact proof/value shelf.
   Use factual non-metric proof until real numbers exist: "Live & online", "Deutschsprachig", "Für Gründer:innen", "Konkrete Workflows". Do not invent counts.

4. Expertise taxonomy before course cards.
   Add a section that explains the four capabilities behind the offer: Work, Build, Launch, Operate. This can make the flagship hierarchy feel more inevitable.

5. Tighter service-card treatment.
   Adapt the thin accent-border card style for course outcomes or deliverables. Keep the existing green accent rather than copying the orange palette.

6. Richer flagship/course cards.
   Borrow the workshop-card density: badges, duration/format/result, bullets, status like "Auf Anfrage", and one clear details CTA. Avoid prices/dates until factual.

7. Dark closing CTA.
   Add a high-contrast final section with two paths: "Flagship anfragen" and "Inhouse Workshop anfragen".

8. Floating back-to-top button.
   Optional, but useful now that the site has several long course pages.

## What Not To Copy Directly

1. Do not copy the exact visual identity.
   The orange/green palette, mascot, type treatment, and portrait composition are part of that site's brand.

2. Do not copy the metrics pattern with invented numbers.
   The pattern is good; the content must stay factual.

3. Do not copy the newsletter/captcha block in this pass.
   It adds external dependencies and friction. JetztAberPROMPT! currently benefits more from direct email CTAs.

4. Do not add cookie/tracking infrastructure unless analytics are actually introduced.
   The consent modal is well structured, but the current static site does not need that overhead.

## Recommended Adaptation Priority

1. Add a stronger personal/profile visual section.
2. Add a Work/Build/Launch/Operate expertise taxonomy before or near the course grid.
3. Make the flagship card more productized with format/result/status details.
4. Add a dark final CTA section.
5. Consider a back-to-top button after the page length is finalized.
