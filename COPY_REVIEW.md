# Prompting Up a Business — Copy Review

Working copy deck for the course page. We will review the rows one by one and only transfer approved language into the website.

## Editorial north star

The strongest version of the offer is not “learn AI” and not merely “build an app.” It is:

> Bring a focused digital product live, then build the feedback, marketing, and operating loops that turn it into a business.

Recommended principles:

- **Concrete before clever:** name the product, PRD, deployment, tests, users, and routines.
- **A credible first version:** promise a deliberately small, testable product—not an entire company in 32 hours.
- **One language system:** English module names can work, but the explanations and outcomes should be plain German.
- **Every repetition has a job:** overview = journey; curriculum = learning; deliverables = artifacts; agenda = sequence; loops = ongoing behavior.
- **AI as leverage, not magic:** show where AI accelerates work and where the participant remains responsible.
- **A clearer next step:** the CTA should say what happens after the click.
